# Сетевой фон Арракиса

Создан встроенным инструментом генерации изображений 2026-10-19.
Ресурс игры: `web/src/gfx/assets/frontend/network-arrakis-observatory-v1.png`.
Используется в `web/src/ui/network.ts`: фон сетевых экранов и матовая подложка планшета.
Текст, кнопки, рамка и состояния комнат рисуются интерфейсом, а не запечены в изображение.

## Полный промпт

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final production background artwork for the multiplayer lobby and settings screens of a modern Dune: Battle for Arrakis strategy-game remaster. Generate a single beautiful cinematic widescreen 16:9 raster image, ideally 2560x1440 or larger. The visual should feel like an imperial tactical command tablet overlooking Arrakis, consistent with detailed realistic RTS remaster art. Scene: an austere futuristic orbital command observatory, overlooking a majestic amber desert planet, immense natural dune ridges, deep canyon silhouettes, suspended spice dust and soft volumetric shafts of warm light through a distant dust storm. Palette: near-black graphite, desaturated blue-gray titanium, restrained warm amber and sand gold; a few very subtle cool-blue navigation glints at the edges. Rich believable physical materials and excellent atmospheric depth, premium game key-art quality, elegant and understated rather than generic neon cyberpunk. Composition is critically for a UI BACKGROUND, not a mockup: the middle 70 percent must be a broad quiet dark graphite negative-space region with extremely low contrast so many live text fields and a room list can be placed over it; put the most beautiful environment detail and amber lighting along the left and right outer edges and the lower edge, with a subtle illuminated planetary atmospheric arc toward the upper-right edge. Peripheral titanium surfaces may suggest a command deck, but do not draw a tablet device, screen border, panels, buttons or fake interface. No people. No text, letters, numbers, logos, emblems, watermarks, labels, charts, grids, interface widgets or readable marks. Full bleed edge to edge, no blank border. This must be a distinctive polished Arrakis multiplayer background asset with a calm readable center, not a screenshot of a finished menu.
